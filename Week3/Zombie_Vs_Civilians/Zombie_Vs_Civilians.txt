Properties of a Zombie:
    1. Runs away if it meets a group of 5+ civilians
    2. Has a normal view radius
    3. Attacks the nearest Civilian and avoids other Zombies

Properties of a Civilian:
    1. Lives forever, unless killed by a Zombie
    2. Has a normal view radius when calm, larger view radius when alert
    3. Tries to flock if alert.
    4. Has a probability (in the video, prob = 1) of getting alerted if nearby civilian is     	alerted.

As a Zombie (Green) moves closer to a Calm Civilian (Blue), the civilian goes into alerted mode (Red), and can share this information with nearby civilians as well. A civilian stays in alerted mode till 10s after the time instant when the zombie is out of its view radius.
A group of 5+ Civilians scare the Zombie away.
